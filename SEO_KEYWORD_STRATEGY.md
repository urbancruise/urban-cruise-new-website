# Urban Cruise SEO Keyword Strategy

This strategy assigns one search intent to each important page family. The
current location routes are Delhi, Gurugram, Mumbai and Pune. The same
framework can be expanded to Jaipur, Chandigarh, Varanasi, Goa, Ahmedabad and
Kolkata only after those locations have dedicated pages and genuinely local
content.

## Keyword governance

- Use the **primary keyword once per page** as the page's main H1 intent.
- Use secondary terms in relevant H2s and body sections, not as repeated
  variations in every paragraph.
- Use one or two long-tail phrases in supporting copy, FAQs or booking CTAs.
- Use the local keyword in the title, description, introduction and at least
  one internal-link anchor where the location is part of the intent.
- Use descriptive image alt text that describes the image first; add a keyword
  only when it accurately describes the image.
- Do not publish a city/service page until its prices, routes, availability,
  landmarks, service area or testimonials are materially local.
- A page must own one primary intent. Link adjacent intents to it instead of
  targeting them repeatedly.

## On-page implementation rules

1. Put the primary keyword in the route's generated title, meta description
   and exactly one H1.
2. Use secondary keywords as descriptive H2s only where the section answers
   that intent.
3. Use long-tail keywords in FAQs, booking copy and route-specific paragraphs.
4. Use local keywords in useful location details, not repeated city mentions.
5. Use semantic terms to broaden topical coverage naturally.
6. Link from city hubs to vehicle/service pages with anchors such as
   “Tempo Traveller Rental in Delhi”, not “click here”.
7. Link from service pages back to the relevant city hub and complementary
   vehicle pages.
8. Keep airport transfer, outstation, corporate and wedding intents on their
   own pages once created; do not make the city homepage rank for all of them.
